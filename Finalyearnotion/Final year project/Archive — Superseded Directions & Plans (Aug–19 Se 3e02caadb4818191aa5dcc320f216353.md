# Archive — Superseded Directions & Plans (Aug–19 Sep 2026)

<aside>
🗄️

**Not the current project.** Everything inside this page belongs to directions and plans that were dropped before the current project — prenatal heart screening from fetal ECG, Doppler ultrasound and echocardiography. It is kept, not deleted, for two reasons: Student Guideline 2 requires a Log Book of meetings, decisions and work done, and General Guideline 3 means every change of title, scope or methodology needs an approval trail.

Do not quote anything from here as current work.

</aside>

## How the project got here

| Date | Direction | Why it changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aug 2026 | Welfare-scheme eligibility system (presented at Review I, 18 Aug) | The Review I panel directed a change and gave the ECG area |
| 7–14 Sep | Digitization of photographed paper ECGs, with a clinical-parameter error metric | 15 Sep: the guide judged ECG digitization and classification common, and asked for an upgrade such as early prediction or a software enhancement |
| 16 Sep | Second-Look ECG — a software layer predicting when the ECG machine's automatic report is wrong | Superseded by the prenatal CHD direction on 19 Sep |
| 17 Sep | Review II held | Panel: use Connected Papers; 1D-CNN papers are already plentiful; medical imaging work nearly always uses U-Net; ECG is already available as a digital signal, so why image-type questions |
| 19 Sep | Prenatal congenital heart disease screening from fetal echocardiography — the "Know When to Refer" plan | Same day, the team decided to use three inputs; the echo-only experiment plan is archived below |
| 19 Sep (evening) | **Prenatal heart screening from fetal ECG + Doppler ultrasound + echocardiography** — the current project | See the hub page and the Methodology Research page |
