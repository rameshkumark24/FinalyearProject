## General Guidelines

1. Project work shall be carried out strictly according to the department-approved project schedule.
2. Students shall work only on the **approved project title, problem statement, and objectives**.
3. Any change in the **project title, scope, methodology, or team members** shall be made only with the **prior approval of the Project Coordinator**.
4. All project activities, discussions, reviews, submissions, and evaluations shall be completed within the specified deadlines.
5. Students are expected to maintain professional discipline, regular attendance, and active participation throughout Phase I.
6. **Each project team shall be required to publish at least one research paper in a Scopus-indexed journal or conference** based on the project work, subject to the applicable institutional guidelines and publication requirements.

## Guidelines for Students

1. Meet the Project Guide regularly and follow the guidance provided.
2. Maintain a **Project Progress Record / Log Book** documenting meetings, discussions, literature survey, work completed, and future plans.
3. During Phase I, complete the following activities:
    - Problem identification and definition
    - Literature survey
    - Identification of research gap / problem gap
    - Problem statement and objectives
    - Proposed methodology
    - System / design architecture, wherever applicable
    - Selection of tools, technologies, datasets, and resources
    - Preliminary implementation / experimental work, wherever applicable
    - Preparation of project documentation
4. Attendance at all project review meetings is **mandatory**.
5. **Every project team member must have a clearly defined individual contribution.**
6. Be prepared to demonstrate understanding of the project work, methodology, references, and individual contribution at every review.
7. All technical information, data, results, diagrams, and references must be properly documented and maintained.
8. Strict adherence to academic integrity and plagiarism guidelines. **Any copied content without proper citation will be considered a violation.**
9. **The use of AI-generated content and external resources must be supported by proper verification, understanding, and appropriate acknowledgement** as per institutional guidelines.
10. All required documents, reports, and presentations must be submitted within the prescribed deadlines.
11. Any changes to the project title, scope, methodology, or major components must receive prior approval from the Project Guide.
12. Non-compliance with review schedules or submission deadlines may result in an impact on the Phase I evaluation and overall project progress assessment.

**How this affects us — data use (planning note).** Heartbeat and CARDIUM are released for **academic and research use only**, through request forms: follow their terms, do not redistribute the images, and cite the datasets. NInFEA, NIFEADB and CinC 2013 are open on PhysioNet under ODC-By 1.0 — cite them. CARDIUM data is CC BY-NC 4.0 and its code Apache-2.0. Heartbeat's code has **no licence file**, so use it to reproduce results but do not redistribute it. FetalCLIP and HuBERT-ECG weights are **non-commercial**. Any Phase II data from an Indian hospital needs institutional ethics committee approval and patient consent; prenatal ultrasound in India falls under the **PCPNDT Act 1994**, so data must come from a registered facility and must carry **no fetal-sex information**. Data-protection rules under India's DPDP Act 2023 should be confirmed with the committee.
